## Group Overview

Zero Mail is built by a 6-member student startup team.

Team composition:

- 3 Developers
- 1 Marketing member
- 2 IB members

Role framing:

- Developers own Gmail integration, backend, frontend, AI triage workflow, system integration, deployment, testing, and technical safety.
- Marketing owns beta recruitment, public content, communication channels, startup/community outreach, and campaign messaging.
- IB owns PM/BA work, business testing, user feedback collection, startup validation, documentation, and stakeholder communication.
- One IB member acts as Project Manager/Scrum Master.

## Product Overview

Zero Mail is a multi-tenant SaaS that helps busy professionals and founders reach inbox zero in Gmail by using AI to auto-triage, categorize, archive, and draft replies based on user-defined natural-language rules.

Target users:

- busy professionals
- founders
- student entrepreneurs
- Gmail / Google Workspace users recruited for beta

Core value:

> AI auto-triage that users can trust with their real Gmail inbox.

## Project Assumptions

- Duration: May 2026 to August 2026
- V1 beta target: May 16, 2026
- Planned beta size: 100 users
- Budget assumption: 42,000,000 VND
- Sponsor context: FPT University as academic/startup sponsor in the course scenario
- Beta model: free beta
- Post-beta business model: prepaid credits / pay-as-you-go credits

## Marketing And Validation Goals

- Recruit 100 beta users
- Onboard at least 80 active beta users
- Collect at least 50 qualified feedback responses
- Reach at least 1,000 targeted prospects through FPT, startup, and community channels
- Convert at least 20 users into post-beta paid-credit intent
- Reach at least 80% positive beta-user satisfaction by the end of beta testing

## V1 Safety Boundaries

Allowed Gmail write actions:

- label
- archive
- save Gmail draft

Forbidden in v1:

- auto-send email
- long-term raw email body storage
- raw LLM prompt storage
- raw LLM completion storage
- embeddings of user mail
- Outlook/Yahoo integration
- mobile app
- enterprise admin console

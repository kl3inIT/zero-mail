Zero Mail does not persist email bodies, LLM prompts, or model completions. Any content sent to an LLM is sanitized, truncated, and hardened against prompt-injection attacks beforehand.

The only write actions permitted in v1 are: label, archive (skip-inbox), and save Gmail draft. **Auto-send is forbidden.**

You can revoke Zero Mail's Gmail access at any time from your Google Account settings.

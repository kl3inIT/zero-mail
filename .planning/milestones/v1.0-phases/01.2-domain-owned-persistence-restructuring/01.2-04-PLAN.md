Move the onboarding domain (entity + repo + enum + service) out of `core.persistence` and `core.account` into `com.zeromail.core.onboarding.{persistence,model,service}.*`. Add the new single-domain delete method `OnboardingService.deleteSelectionsForCurrentTenant(UUID)` per CL-2. Flip `UserEntity`'s `OnboardingStep` import to the new location. Add the Wave 0 net-new `OnboardingStepEnumPersistenceTest` to defend against Pitfall 5 (enum-name persistence drift).

Purpose: Onboarding is the second-largest domain move and the one that breaks the symmetric back-edge in the dependency graph (`account ↔ onboarding`). By doing this after the account move, the executor is working from a known-good baseline (Plan 03 left AccountDeletionE2ETest green) and can incrementally verify each substitution.

Output: 4 source relocations across 3 sub-packages; 4 new package-info markers; 1 amended account/package-info; 1 net-new persistence test; OnboardingService gains its delete method; UserEntity import flipped; AccountDeletionController transitional bridge progresses one step toward final form.
</objective>

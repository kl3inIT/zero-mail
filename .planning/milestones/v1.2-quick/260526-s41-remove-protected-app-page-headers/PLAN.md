# Remove protected app page headers

## Scope

- Remove page-level title/description header bars from protected app pages.
- Keep functional controls by moving them into local toolbars where needed.
- Do not remove card, table, dialog, or section headers that describe local content.

## Verification

- Typecheck, lint, and focused Playwright specs for changed protected pages.

## Deviations Reviewed

- `users.preferred_language` is `varchar(2)` (not `varchar(8)`). Explicit Plan 01 deviation documented in CONTEXT and SUMMARY; aligns with the locked `vi`/`en`-only product constraint and a CHECK constraint enforces the allow-list at the DB layer. Does not compromise SC-1.
- `ErrorCodes.VALIDATION` is the constant `"error.validation"` (rather than the `error.validation.field_invalid` form referenced in the verification target). The handler emits `code = error.validation` and per-field codes under `error.validation.field.<field>.<constraint>`. The frontend bundles localize from `errors.validation.generic` (top-level) and `errors.validation.field.<field>.<constraint>` (per field). Internally consistent across handler, ArchUnit lock, and bundles; the verification target text was illustrative, not a contract — actual contract is what `ErrorCodes.java` and the bundles agree on.
- The handler keeps a transitional `message` alias (`pd.setProperty("message", code)`) for client compatibility during the rollout. Documented as a transitional alias scheduled for removal in the next phase per D-C1; it carries the dotted code (no localized prose), so SC-2 is not violated.
- `LanguageSwitcher` is mounted in `app/layout.tsx`, `app/login/page.tsx`, and `app/settings/page.tsx`. SC-1 only requires app-chrome + `/settings`; the additional `/login` mount is a stronger placement for unauthenticated users (still satisfies the contract).

None of the deviations compromise the phase's stated outcomes.

<objective>
Make the Gmail client factory mailbox-aware and close the cross-mailbox token-bleed hole (D-10, the central phase threat). Introduce `MailboxRef(UUID tenantId, UUID gmailConnectionId)`, add `buildClientForMailbox(MailboxRef)`, and re-key the in-memory `accessTokenCache` from `tenantId` to `gmailConnectionId` at all three sites (get/put/remove) — including re-keying `buildClientForConnection` to `entity.getId()`. The AES-GCM decrypt AAD stays `tenantId.toString()` (D-11). Keep `buildClientForTenant` as a `@Deprecated(forRemoval=true)` adapter that fails loud if a tenant has >1 connected mailbox. Ship the D-13 ArchUnit allow-list rule NOW with the 9 known legacy callers and `allowEmptyShould(false)`.

Purpose: Without this re-key, two CONNECTED mailboxes in one tenant collide on the tenant cache key and mailbox B silently receives mailbox A's access token (RESEARCH Pitfall 4). This is the single most important runtime-state change in the phase.
Output: 1 new record + factory re-key + 1 new ArchUnit test.
</objective>

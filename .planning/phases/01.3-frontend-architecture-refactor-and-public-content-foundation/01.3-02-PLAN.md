Why this exact shape:
- Entry 1 (TS/TSX/JS/JSX): unchanged — runs ESLint then Prettier on staged JS-family files.
- Entry 2 (apps/web/messages/*.json): runs Prettier ONCE, then i18n:check **warn-only**
  (the trailing `|| true` makes the hook chain pass even when i18n:check exits non-zero
  on intentional Plan 05/06 drift). Plan 07 final task removes the `|| true`.
- Entry 3 (apps/web/!(messages)/**/*.{md,mdx,json,css}): broad formatter for nested
  paths, EXCLUDING the messages directory via the `!(messages)` extglob negation.
  Prevents the duplicate-run race that the original config had.
- Entry 4 (apps/web/*.{md,json,css}): handles top-level apps/web files (package.json,
  next.config-ish files at the root of apps/web that aren't covered by entry 3 because
  entry 3 requires at least one nested directory).
